# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students sitting Pearson Edexcel International A Level (IAL) exams, first in the Maldives (mostly 16–19). They revise
around school, often in the evening, on phones as much as laptops, against fixed exam sessions (January, May/June,
October/November). Their job: find out what they don't know yet and fix it before the exam.

## Product Purpose

Study-IAL lets students practise questions organised by specification topic, turns their marks into a per-topic mastery
score, and uses that score to tell them what to revise next — on the dashboard and through a day-by-day study plan built
around their exam dates. Success: a student opens it, sees their weakest topic, and practises it.

## Positioning

Practice and progress are organised the way the IAL specification is (unit → topic, with real paper codes such as
WPH11), not by past paper, and the product always answers "what should I revise next?" from the student's own marks.
It also carries local Maldivian subjects (Islam, Dhivehi) alongside IAL ones.

## Operating Context

Revision sessions with mark schemes, specification checklists, exam countdowns, Pomodoro-style focus blocks, and
self-marking against a mark scheme.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Curriculum browser (subject → unit → topic), practice sessions (MCQ auto-marked; written answers self-marked or
AI-marked), mastery per topic, adaptive study plans with rescheduling, focus timer with streaks, admin question bank.
Free to use. Not affiliated with Pearson Edexcel; past papers are linked, not re-hosted.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Study-IAL. Voice: plain, encouraging, specific; sentence case. Homepage copy lives in `app/page.tsx` and `components/landing/marketing-parts.tsx`.

## Evidence on Hand

Real content: 175 specification topics across Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics and Further Mathematics; 114
original Physics practice questions. No testimonials, user counts, press or partner logos exist — do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Always point to the next useful thing to revise.
- Organise by the specification, in the IAL's own vocabulary.
- Honest progress: mastery comes from the student's own recent marks.
- Calm under exam pressure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast; works at 375px wide; respects reduced motion; usable on modest phones and connections.
