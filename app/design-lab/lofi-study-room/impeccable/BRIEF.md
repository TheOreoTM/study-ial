## Shape (assumptions: no user available; brief + PRODUCT.md treated as confirmed answers)

- **Job and audience:** IAL students (16–19, Maldives) arriving in the evening, often on a phone, unsure what to revise.
  Mode: **Persuade**. They should believe the product tells them what to revise next, then press "Start practising free".
- **Outcome and proof:** the WPH11 mastery example is the proof — four real topic codes, honest scores, the weakest one
  cued as next. Stats 175 / 114 / Free. No invented claims, testimonials, or counts.
- **Selected direction:** world pinned by the brief (Lo-fi Study Room: warm dusk, lamp glow, terracotta/plum/mustard,
  rounded type). Structure dealt by concept-seed (surface scope, lead = candidate 7 of 7): **the tape shelf** — the
  study room's objects carry the content: the mastery example is a cassette deck whose tracklist is the unit's topics,
  the cued track is the weakest topic; subjects are cassette cases stacked flat on a shelf; the hero sits in front of a dusk window.
- **Scope:** one static page, all copy from `design-lab/content.ts` verbatim; code-led (no image generation available).
- **Anti-goals:** notebook/paper/ruled lines/handwriting; cream-paper-plus-serif; neon-on-black; kicker labels; identical
  icon cards as the whole structure; gamified hype.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is the room you revise in at dusk; the product proof is the tape in the deck — a unit's topics
as a tracklist, the weakest one cued to play next. Refuses the category default of hero text + floating dashboard
screenshot + feature-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Dusk plum walls (#2A1D2E), a window of terracotta-to-mustard sky over a dark sea with a palm, one warm lamp
pool of light. Tape objects: rounded cassette bodies, two reels, label strips; segmented level meters for mastery
(sage strong, mustard developing, terracotta weak). Fredoka display, M PLUS Rounded 1c text. Soft, offset, warm shadows.
The single brightest lamp glow is reserved for the cued (next-up) track.

STORY: Visitor sees the room, reads the headline, sees WPH11's tracklist with 1.5 cued and the nextUp line, learns the
three-step loop, scans six features, finds their subject on the shelf, closes on the sunset band with the CTA.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: left 45% — headline (~5rem), subtext, CTAs (mustard pill primary, outline
secondary), then the audience line + stats strip below a dotted rule (no eyebrow). Right 55% — arched dusk window, the cassette-deck card overlapping its lower half.
Lamp hangs top-left, its glow falling on the headline. Mobile: text first, deck card below, window becomes a short
band behind the card.
